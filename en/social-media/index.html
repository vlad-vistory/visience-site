What you should know about social media management

Which networks do you work on?

We run Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn accounts. We recommend the right ones for your audience and the kind of business you are in. There is no point being everywhere — only where your customers are. We settle the mix together at the strategy stage.

What does a post include?

Every post includes copy written for your brand and the graphic design (or working up material you already have), plus the relevant hashtags and scheduling at the right hour. On the full-management packages we add strategic comments and interaction with your followers.

How long until results show?

Social is a channel that builds over time. The first signs — interaction going up, follower count growing — usually appear within 1–2 months, and solid results in community and awareness settle in over 3–6 months of steady presence. That is why consistency matters more than any single post.

Is there a minimum contract?

The monthly packages work best with a commitment of a few months, because results come from consistency, not from one month. We go through the actual terms at the start and shape them around your goals, with no surprise clauses.

Do you produce the photo and video content too?

We do the graphic design and work up the material you give us. For a dedicated photo shoot or video production, we coordinate with our partners and quote it separately. Plenty of brands start with existing material and photos from a phone, which we turn into professional posts.

How do you report results?

You get a clear monthly report with the numbers that matter: follower growth, engagement, post reach and which kind of content performed best. On Full SMM we add a 1:1 consulting call to go through the results and the next steps.
