What you should know about branding

How much does a professional logo cost?

A professional logo starts at $600 (the Logo Basic package), which covers an original logo, one round of revisions, the mono and reversed versions plus the versions with and without a tagline, in every format. If you want a complete visual identity, the branding packages run from $1,400 to $2,900, depending on how much material is included.

What is a brand book and what is in it?

The brand book is the document that sets the rules for using your visual identity. It holds the correct logo versions, the colour palette (with the exact colour codes), the typography, the spacing and examples of correct and incorrect use. So whoever builds you a website, a poster or a post later on keeps the brand consistent. It is included in the Premium package.

What formats do I get the logo in?

You get the logo in every format you need: vector (for print and for resizing with no loss of quality), PNG with a transparent background, JPG and PDF. We also deliver the colour, mono and reversed versions, so you always have the right file for the job — from a business card to a lit sign.

How many revisions are included?

It depends on the package: Logo Basic includes 1 round, Branding Standard 3 rounds, and Branding Premium 6 rounds. A round means one set of adjustments to the direction you chose, to bring it exactly to the point where you are happy with it.

How long does a branding project take?

It depends on the package and on how quickly we get feedback on the proposals. We fix the date in the quote, after the brief.

Do you do rebranding for an existing company?

Yes. We often work with established companies whose image no longer matches the quality of what they do. We look at the current identity, keep what works and rebuild the rest, so the change reads as a step forward rather than a break that confuses loyal customers. We can then update the website and the material too, so the transition is complete.
