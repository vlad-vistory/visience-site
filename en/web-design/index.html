What clients
ask

How much does a business website cost?
A business website starts at $870 — the Basic package, with up to 6 pages. The Standard package, with full on-page SEO, is $1,400, and a premium site with custom design starts at $2,900. For a single campaign, a landing page starts at $600. The final price depends on the number of pages, the features and how much of it is custom.
How long does a website take?
A landing page is ready in a few days. A business website usually takes 2–4 weeks, a premium project 4–8 weeks, depending on how quickly we get the text and the photos. You get the exact date in the quote, in writing, and we keep to it.
What platform do you build on?
Most often WordPress (Oxygen Builder or Elementor) and Webflow. We pick the platform to suit the project, so you can edit the content easily after handover.
Will the site be optimised for phones and for Google?
Yes. Every site is responsive and has technical SEO built in: speed, proper structure and structured data. We put the weight on mobile speed, since that is where most visitors arrive.
Can I edit the content myself after handover?
Yes. Every site includes a CMS, so you can change text, images and pages with no technical knowledge. We also run a training session at handover.
Do you write the copy as well?
Yes. Conversion-focused copywriting is included in the Standard and Premium packages, and can be added as an option on a landing page.
