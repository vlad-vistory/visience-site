What does a software project cost?

We work at a standard rate of $70 an hour across all IT services, and the final price of each project is set after we have looked at the requirements and the technical complexity. A small automation project might mean a few days of work, while a full ERP system or a mobile app runs over several months. What matters is that you get a detailed, honest estimate before we start, with no surprises along the way.

The first conversation and the initial analysis are free. We look at what you need together, we tell you straight whether it is worth building custom or whether something off the shelf fits better, and only then do we send the effort and budget estimate. See the rate and what is included below →

Scalable code, documented, and 100% yours. Unlike off-the-shelf products, custom software moulds to your processes and connects cleanly to what you already run: invoicing, ERP, internal tools. That means fewer compromises, real operational efficiency and lower costs over the long run.

What you should know about
your software project

How is the price of a software project worked out?

We work at a standard rate of $70 an hour across all IT services. The final price of a project is set after we look at the requirements and the technical complexity, expressed as an effort estimate in hours. You get that detailed estimate before we start, so the budget and the timeline are clear.

What technologies do you use?

We pick the technology to suit the project, not the other way round. We work with modern stacks for web, mobile and backend, cloud on AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, relational and NoSQL databases, plus AI and data science tools where they genuinely add something. We recommend the architecture after the initial analysis.

Who owns the source code?

You own it outright. At the end of the project you get the whole codebase and the technical documentation with it, so you are not dependent on us. You can carry on with any team, whenever you want.

Do you offer maintenance after handover?

Yes. After launch we provide support and maintenance for updates, bug fixes and further development, at the same rate of $70 an hour. We agree a way of working together — ad hoc or on a retainer — depending on what you need.

Is the initial analysis really free?

Yes, the first conversation and the initial analysis of the project are entirely free, with nothing to sign. We look at what you need, tell you honestly how it can be built, and only then send the effort and budget estimate.

Do you build native mobile apps too?

Yes. We build native mobile apps for iOS and Android, as well as cross-platform solutions, depending on the goals and the budget. We handle the whole process, from analysis and design through to publishing on the App Store and Google Play.
