What the page that brings the leads looks like

The campaign takes people to one place, and that is where everything is decided. The structure below is the one that brought the 600 leads for Armur Design: exactly as many sections as it takes to answer every question, in the order the questions come up.

01

The opening

The name of the development, the city, how many homes and how much land. In three seconds someone knows whether this is for them, without scrolling.

02

What they get

Design, comfort, materials, energy efficiency. Four short arguments with icons, not three paragraphs nobody reads.

03

Concept and photography

What kind of home this is and who it is for, with the gallery of renders underneath. This is where it stops looking like a portal listing.

04

The enquiry button

The same request appears several times down the page, so nobody has to hunt for where to leave their details once they have decided.

05

Details and floor plans

Room-by-room areas, layout, floor plan. Exactly what gets asked on the first phone call, written down once for everyone.

06

Materials and specification

Structure, façade, utilities, garden. This section convinces the serious buyers and stops the ones who were never going to buy.

Location on the map

Real distances to schools, shops and the centre, not just an address written out.

Contact form

On the same page, short, with the details going straight into the CRM.

What developers ask us most often

How soon can I launch the website for my development?

It depends on how many apartment types go on the site and how quickly we receive the plans and renders. We fix the date in the quote and we keep to it.

How much do I spend a month on Google and Meta Ads?

The ad budget is NOT part of our packages — you pay it straight to Google and Meta. For a mid-size scheme we recommend at least $1,160 a month on Google plus $930 a month on Meta. As a starting split we put 60% on Google (searches with intent) and 40% on Meta, then move budget each month towards whichever channel has the lower cost per lead.

Do you have experience with mortgage-financed sales?

Yes. All our sites include pages on the payment options, a mortgage calculator and banking partners. We work with mortgage brokers to build an interactive calculator into the site.

Can you work alongside my PR agency or my estate agent?

Yes, we often work in extended teams. We coordinate with the estate agent on lead handover, with PR on message consistency, and with photographers and 3D studios on the visual assets.

What happens after the last unit sells? Do I still need the site?

We keep the site live for the brand's credibility — buyers want to check it. We cut the ad budget to a minimum. If you launch the next phase or project, the start is easy: the brand and the CRM are already there.

Do you work with developers outside Romania?

Yes. We have clients in Romania, Italy, France and Germany, and we work with developers in the United States. The whole engagement can run remotely — meetings on Zoom or Meet work just as well.

What does marketing for a residential development cost?

Packages start at $4,060 one-off for small developments (starter branding plus website). The one most people take is Standard: from $7,540 setup plus $1,740 a month, with campaigns and reporting included. The package prices are published, on this page. The ad budget is paid separately, straight to Google and Meta, and the area analysis at stage 01 costs $930, deducted from the launch package if we go ahead.

Do you produce photorealistic 3D renders?

We do not produce 3D renders in house. We take the ones made by your architect, or coordinate a specialist partner, then work them into the website, the campaigns and the sales material. You keep one point of contact, and we stay on what we do best: the strategy and the leads.

How do we sell apartments before construction is finished?

Off-plan selling needs trust: a website with clear plans and the build progress updated monthly, a waiting list with a launch offer, awareness campaigns in the area, and automated email that keeps leads warm until the reservation contract. That is the model we run on every residential project.

Do you work with estate agencies and listing portals?

Yes. We coordinate listings on portals such as Imobiliare.ro and Storia, with separate tracking per channel, and hand the leads to your agents or your partner agency through the CRM. You see exactly where each buyer came from and which channel deserves the budget.
