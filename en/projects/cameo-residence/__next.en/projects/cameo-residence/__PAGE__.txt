The task was to turn the quality of the development into an online presence that inspires the same confidence — and to put it in front of the people actually looking for a flat in Iași. We handled the whole chain: <strong>brand, website, campaigns, social media and printed material</strong>.</p>

        <div class="cs-svc__n">03 — Facebook &amp; Google Ads</div>
        <h3>Real buyers, at a low cost per lead</h3>
        <p>We started campaigns on Google and Meta aimed at people searching for new flats in Iași, with creative showing life in the development, not just the building. The budget was moved monthly towards the channel with the lowest cost per lead — and the result showed in phone calls and viewings, not only in clicks.</p>
        <p>The ads do not say "flats for sale". Each one takes a single feature and opens it up: the Porta Vector door, the Rehau underfloor heating, the Barlinek flooring. Over the photograph we put the technical section of the product, so people see what it is made of, not just what it looks like. It is the kind of ad somebody comparing two developments stops on.</p>

        <div class="cs-svc__n">04 — Social media</div>
        <h3>A series, not scattered posts</h3>
        <p>We built the posts as a system, starting from the real features of the development: the solar system, the charging points for electric cars, the OPAL RC2 entrance door, the Schindler 3000 lift, the Viessmann boiler, the Barlinek flooring. One post, one feature, with the specification written exactly as it is on the data sheet — output, security class, warranty.</p>
        <p>The layout is the same every time: the line pointing at the element in the photograph, the name of the feature at the top, the benefit at the bottom. No prices appear, and the word "premium" does not appear. After a few posts, the development is recognisable in a feed without having to read the name.</p>
