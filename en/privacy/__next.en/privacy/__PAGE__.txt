<p>On 25 May 2018 European Regulation 2016/679 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data (below, the GDPR) came into force. www.visience.ro is committed to protecting individual privacy and personal data.</p>

<p>This privacy policy applies to the website www.visience.ro.</p>

<h2>2. Principles</h2>
<p>www.visience.ro is committed to the principles set out in the GDPR, and accordingly personal data is:</p>
<p>Processed lawfully, fairly and transparently in relation to the data subject;<br/>Collected for specified, explicit and legitimate purposes and not further processed in a way incompatible with those purposes;<br/>Adequate, relevant and limited to what is necessary in relation to the purposes for which it is processed;<br/>Accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date;<br/>Kept in a form which permits identification of data subjects for no longer than is necessary for the purposes for which the data is processed;<br/>Processed in a way that ensures appropriate security of the personal data.</p>

<h2>3. Categories of data processed</h2>
<p>www.visience.ro collects the personal data of users, members, website visitors and clients or prospective clients. Where the law requires it, www.visience.ro gives individuals appropriate notice of the data that will be collected and how it will be used.</p>
<p>We collect only the personal data that individuals provide voluntarily, so that we can supply information, deliver services, carry out contractual relationships and promote our services. In addition, we have to process certain personal data in order to provide our services or to comply with various legal obligations.</p>
<p>Data processed: identification data (first name, last name), telephone number, email address, address.</p>

<h2>4. Processing activities</h2>
<p>Registering in the www.visience.ro system: to create a www.visience.ro account, and thereby become a www.visience.ro user, data subjects must provide certain personal data such as name, telephone number and email address;<br/>Requesting a quote: in order to obtain a quote or information about the services provided by www.visience.ro, clients, prospective clients or their representatives must provide data such as first name, last name and contact details;<br/>Visitors to www.visience.ro: the website uses small text files called cookies, to make the experience work better for users. Some cookies are necessary for the site to run. Visiting our site may involve processing personal data such as: IP address, time and date of access, and similar.<br/>In addition, on the basis of express consent, www.visience.ro may also process cookies such as:</p>
<p>Preference cookies: allow the website to remember information that changes how the site behaves, such as the selected language;<br/>Statistics cookies: help www.visience.ro understand how visitors use the site, by collecting data anonymously;<br/>Marketing cookies: are used to track visitors across websites. The intention is to display ads that are relevant and interesting to each user, and therefore more valuable to publishers and third-party advertisers.</p>
<p>Record of enquiries: requests sent through the forms on the site are kept in an internal record, hosted on the www.visience.ro server, so that we can answer the request and follow the conversation through to its conclusion. Alongside the contact details you leave us, we also keep information about the visit that led you to write: the page you arrived on, the page and the area of the page you started from towards the form, the source of the visit (search engine, social network, campaign) and the device type. This information is used strictly internally, to understand which content is useful to visitors, and is not passed to any other person or company.</p>
<p>Form progress and page feedback: to see where visitors run into difficulty, we record, with no personal data at all, the steps taken in a form (it was seen, filling in started, it was submitted) and the answers to the question "Was this page useful?". <h2>7. How long data is kept</h2>
<p>www.visience.ro stores data only for as long as the basis that justified the processing continues to exist.</p>
