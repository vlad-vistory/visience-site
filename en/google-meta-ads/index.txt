      <details class="ads-faq-item">
        <summary>What is the minimum advertising budget?</summary>
        <p>The media budget is paid straight to Google and Meta and sits outside the management fee. As a starting point we recommend <strong>at least $600 a month</strong> per platform, and for steady results the <strong>$930–$1,740 a month</strong> range works best. The larger and steadier the budget, the lower the cost per lead tends to go, because the algorithms have more data to work with. We recommend the exact figure once we understand your business and your market.</p>
      </details>

      <details class="ads-faq-item">
        <summary>Does the management fee include the ad budget?</summary>
        <p><strong>No.</strong> The management fee (<strong>$460 a month per platform</strong>) and the one-off setup (<strong>$460</strong>) cover the strategy, the ad creative, the implementation, the optimisation and the reporting. The <strong>advertising budget</strong> — the money actually spent on ads — is paid straight to Google and Meta from your own account and is completely separate from what you pay us. That way you keep full control of the media budget and can see exactly where it goes.</p>
      </details>

      <details class="ads-faq-item">
        <summary>Which platforms do you run campaigns on?</summary>
        <p>We run campaigns on <strong>Google Ads</strong> (Search, Display, Shopping, YouTube), on <strong>Facebook Ads</strong> and on <strong>Instagram Ads</strong>. We pick the combination based on where your customers actually are: Google for people already searching for a service or a product, Meta to reach them first and to remarket. For online stores, we recommend Google Shopping to catch buyers with high intent.</p>
      </details>

      <details class="ads-faq-item">
        <summary>How long until the first results?</summary>
        <p>Campaigns can bring the first clicks and leads within <strong>a few days</strong> of launch. That said, the first 2–3 weeks are a learning period: we test ads, audiences and bids to find what works. Stable results and a settled cost per lead usually come after <strong>4–6 weeks</strong> of running and continuous optimisation.</p>
      </details>

      <details class="ads-faq-item">
        <summary>Do you work with online stores (Shopping)?</summary>
        <p>Yes. For online stores we set up <strong>Google Shopping</strong>: Google Merchant Center, product feed optimisation, Smart or Standard Shopping campaigns and a bidding strategy built for profit. We combine Shopping with remarketing and abandoned-cart email to recover the sales that would otherwise be lost. If you do not have a store yet, we can also help with <a href="/en/online-store/">building an online store</a>.</p>
      </details>

      <details class="ads-faq-item">
        <summary>How do you report performance?</summary>
        <p>You get a <strong>clear monthly report</strong>, with no jargon: impressions, clicks, cost per click, leads or sales and cost per lead. We do not hide behind vanity numbers — we look at what actually matters for your business. We explain what we optimised, what worked and what we are proposing for the month ahead.</p>
